# Capture guide (what the pipeline needs)

Reconstruction only works if the footage overlaps. Ordinary phone video does not contain 360° panoramas; the viewer labels such viewpoints "Reconstructed camera view", never "360° panorama".

1. Film one continuous walkthrough, 1–3 minutes, landscape, at walking pace or slower.
2. Good light, no motion blur. Avoid long shots of blank walls; keep textured objects in view.
3. Enter each room, pause, and slowly pan left and right across the whole room before leaving. Keep doorways in frame when you pass through.
4. Re-visit the hall between rooms so loops can close.
5. For true 360° tours, use a 360° camera and upload equirectangular (2:1) footage, ticking "360° video". Each viewpoint then becomes a real panorama.
6. For measurements, place an object of known size (a ruler, an A4 sheet, a card) in view and use the calibration tools.
7. If the coverage report lists missing time ranges, film those parts again.

If 3D reconstruction reports that no initial camera pair could be found, the video did not provide enough overlapping detail and camera translation for a stable model. Keep textured surfaces visible and move forward or sideways between nearby views; rotating in place, fast turns, and blank walls do not provide reliable 3D geometry. The frame-based viewpoint tour remains available, but ordinary footage is never presented as a 360° panorama.
